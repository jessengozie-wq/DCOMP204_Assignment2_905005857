Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a landlord?

All listed landlords undergo physical verification and document checks by our local team before their listing is approved.

Is there a booking fee?

No, student registration and direct contact with verified landlords on our platform is completely free of charge.

What if a listing turns out to be fake?

Report the listing immediately using the contact page or flag button, and our safety team will investigate within 24 hours.
